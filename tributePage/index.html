Life and Achievements

Marie Skłodowska Curie (1867-1934) was a Polish and naturalized-French physicist and chemist who conducted pioneering research on radioactivity.

Key Milestones:

  • 1867 - Born in Warsaw, Poland
  • 1891 - Moved to Paris to study at the Sorbonne
  • 1895 - Married Pierre Curie
  • 1898 - Discovered polonium and radium
  • 1903 - Awarded Nobel Prize in Physics (shared with Pierre Curie and Henri Becquerel)
  • 1911 - Awarded Nobel Prize in Chemistry
  • 1934 - Died from aplastic anemia, likely caused by radiation exposure
"Nothing in life is to be feared, it is only to be understood. Now is the time to understand more, so that we may fear less."
